Frequently asked questions

Is VideoFetch free?

Yes. There are no fees, no sign-up and no watermarks.

How do I download many videos at once?

Use the Bulk links tab to paste up to 200 URLs, or the Playlist / Channel scrape tab to pull every video from a playlist, channel or profile. Select the ones you want and click Download selected (ZIP).

Can I download only the audio?

Yes — choose MP3 or M4A from the quality menu.

Why did a link fail?

Private, age-restricted, region-locked or login-only videos can't be fetched without cookies. The server admin can configure a cookies file to enable these.

Is it legal?

Only download content you own or have permission to download, and respect each platform's terms of service and copyright law.
